Transparency

Where the money went: every organization and person who received funds, with their contact, so you can follow up directly.

Houston

$28,693 raised and distributed

01 / 10

Somos Nexos Venezuela

Connecting needs with goodwill to rebuild.

Provides direct on-the-ground support, developing and coordinating immediate housing projects for families who lost their homes, and connecting urgent needs with people, organizations, and resources ready to help.

Funds allocated $2,500

via Patricia Díaz — @somos.nexosve

02 / 10

GAVSA — San Antonio Volunteer Support Group

Strengthening the community response in La Guaira.

Luis Itanare has supported affected communities since the first week after the earthquake and is now organizing a formal volunteer network in San Antonio. The funds went toward the purchase of a pickup truck to transport supplies and essential goods, which will also be available to the other organizations supported by this fundraiser.

Its usefulness will be reviewed after one year; if it is no longer needed, it will be sold and the proceeds distributed among three community assistance organizations in La Guaira.

Funds allocated $8,000

via @marianameneses CGraterol @mpeach and @kuiz

03 / 10

CECODAP

Protecting the rights and well-being of children and adolescents.

Provides direct support and care to children and adolescents, strengthening their protection and well-being, especially in communities and families facing emergencies and vulnerability.

Funds allocated $2,000

via @beatrizbellorinart and Carlos Trapani — @cecodap

04 / 10

Fondo Resurge

Helping entrepreneurs start over.

Provides financial support to small business owners who lost their tools, equipment, or means of work in the earthquake, helping them restart their businesses, recover their income, and rebuild their livelihoods.

Funds allocated $2,500

via 3 Pasos — @fondoresurge

05 / 10

Agora Leaders

Supporting the emotional recovery of children and families after the earthquake.

Channels resources into psychological care and emotional support programs for children and families affected by the earthquake, working alongside local organizations to support their recovery and well-being.

Funds allocated $2,400

via @beatrizbellorinart @agoravenezuela

06 / 10

FundaGato

Protecting the most vulnerable animals.

Provides shelter, food, and care for abandoned and vulnerable cats, and mobilizes resources to support the care and protection of animals affected by the earthquake.

Impact: 116 cats helped

Funds allocated $1,177

via Luis Castaldi — @fundagato

07 / 10

Mujeres en Escena

Living and caring after the earthquake.

Provides direct support to women, children, and families who lost their homes, distributing essential supplies and running art and well-being workshops for children and adolescents in shelters as spaces for support and emotional recovery.

Impact: 310 children received meals during the workshops, and 310 backpacks with personal hygiene kits were delivered.

Funds allocated $2,746

via Paula — @mujeresenescena.ve

08 / 10

Direct support for rescuers, volunteers, and affected families

Funds were allocated to cover medical and mobility needs, sustain the work of rescuers and volunteers, and respond to individual cases of families affected by the earthquake.

  • Prosthesis for Mr. Luis Palencia$538
  • Financial support and transportation to Mérida for four volunteers$660
  • Medical tests for Mrs. Kenya: spirometry, chest X-ray, and Profile 20 blood panel$150
  • Jerotshi, a volunteer from Margarita: maintenance and oil change for her vehicle$150
  • Rescuer Paola’s truck: brake repair and replacement of shock absorbers and control arms on the vehicle she has used for relief work since the second day after the earthquake$2,710
  • Four new tires for a volunteer’s vehicle, used to drive a girl from La Guaira to Caracas for medical treatment$220
  • Alan, a child who survived the earthquake: medical supplies for his surgery after he was injured by a collapsing wall$226
  • Teacher Elizabeth and Oriana’s mother: financial support for medical care and for a family whose home has structural damage$700
  • SotaEventos: support for educational workshops for the La Guaira community$1,000
Funds allocated $6,354

via @petricorsalvaje, @fabianaelena_, @textvra @superchiquilucky @paolaoraab

09 / 10

Fundación Sonriendo con Uriel

Feeding and supporting affected communities.

Provides food, water, and direct support to children and families in La Guaira affected by the earthquake, organizing community meal days and activities for the children’s well-being and recreation.

Impact: a community meal for 250 people and a special hot dog event for 80 children.

Funds allocated $516

via Paola Oraab and Junior — @fsonriendoconurielj

10 / 10

Wheels for Happiness

Mobility that restores independence and hope.

Provides wheelchairs and mobility equipment to children, young people, and adults with disabilities or reduced mobility, supporting their independence, access to everyday activities, and participation in their communities.

Funds allocated $500

via @rosaanaorlando

It has been a very intense journey, getting to know some incredibly difficult individual cases closely, but also a huge relief to encounter so many brave and generous people who have not stopped working to help those in need. This fundraising effort is dedicated to all of you—to those who have been there every day, supporting communities, finding solutions, and continuing to help despite the enormous challenges.

I’m very pleased to share with you how we distributed the $28,693 raised through our fundraiser, which so many of you supported and participated in. Thank you so much for your trust, generosity, and support. None of this would have been possible without everyone who donated artwork, purchased work, contributed funds, volunteered, or helped us along the way.

We are still amazed and deeply moved by your response. Thank you again for helping us turn this collective effort into direct support for communities affected by the earthquake in Venezuela. ❤️

If you have any questions or concerns, please don’t hesitate to contact us: artistsforvenezuela@gmail.com
